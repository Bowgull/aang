Privacy policy

Aang, a personal desktop assistant used only by Joshua Bocas. Last updated 22 September 2026.

Who uses it

Aang has exactly one user: Joshua Bocas, who built it. It is not offered to the public. The only Google account it connects to is his own.

What it can access

With his consent through Google's own sign-in, Aang asks for:

  • Gmail, read-only: to find and read his messages when he asks, and for a short daily summary of unread mail.
  • Gmail, compose: to prepare drafts. A draft is shown to him in full, and nothing is sent unless he approves that exact wording.
  • Google Calendar, read-only: to tell him what is on today.

Where the data goes

  • The sign-in tokens are stored only on his own computer.
  • When he asks Aang about his mail or calendar, the relevant text is sent to Anthropic's Claude to produce the answer, under his own Anthropic account, and the answer is shown to him on his computer or in his own private Discord server.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, used for advertising, or used to train models by this app.
  • Attachments are listed by name and never opened.

Google API data

Aang's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from Google account permissions, and deleting the token file on his computer removes Aang's copy.
