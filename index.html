Aang

A personal desktop assistant, built and used by one person: Joshua Bocas.

Aang is a small animated companion that lives on Joshua's own computer. He can ask it things, and it can do things for him on that computer: open apps and links, manage files, set reminders, and keep track of his work sessions.

With his permission, Aang can read his own Gmail inbox and Google Calendar so it can tell him what is coming up, and it can prepare email drafts. It never sends an email unless he approves that exact message himself.

Aang is not a product or a service. It is not offered to anyone else, has no other users, and no sign-up.
